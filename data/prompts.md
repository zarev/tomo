# Throxy Outbound Pipeline Prompts

## stage-fit: Stage Fit Gate
Use the company stage to narrow the list to the best initial buyers. Focus on decision-makers and teams who feel the pain of outbound infrastructure early. Explain why each lead is a fit for a pre-seed company building this tooling.

## persona-fit: Persona Fit Filter
Use the ideal persona description to keep only leads who match the target roles, responsibilities, and pain points. Reject leads that are unlikely to champion or buy the product.

## company-fit: Throxy Value Alignment
Use Throxy's company profile to confirm industry, motion, and value alignment. Keep the smallest, highest intent audience that should be prioritized for outbound.

## final-review: Final Target Review
Make a final pass to ensure the remaining list is laser-focused and ready for outreach. Remove any lead that feels speculative or off-target.
